To construct the Commercial catch by year table, we combined:
- Dataset Frankenstein-together year-by-year using the following:
1. area8_mixedstock_sockeye_agedata_1971_to_1987.xlsx- data entered sourced from hardcopy age cards scanned and uploaded to PADs. Found during KM MSc research to find FSC data. Only a few years were used as limited cards were available here. 
2. marine_harvest_age.csv. These are the original commercial ages pulled together by Matt Mortimer and Steve Cox-Rogers. These data were often used as default if overlap was found with #1 source (b/c it is generally more comprehensive and complete). When sources could be determined and one set was missing something, both were included. 
3. Age Readings Data Feb 2026.xlsx-- to verify existing data from source #2. No additional sources were found outside of this dataset.

Limitations with Commercial age data: these are catch ages. Other populations would be included in these catches, thus these age samples. The commercial catch distribution does not necessarily reflect Atnarko sockeye age class distribution. This includes terminal fisheries. Specifically the River-type Atnarko sockeye are NOT included in the stock aggregate being assessed, thus even the BCGNA and FSC samples contain other populations outside of this analysis.
